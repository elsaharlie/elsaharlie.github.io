Meet the ChefThe Chef’s Story

From the pastry kitchen to performance marketing: strategies built on precision, creativity and data.

I’m Elsa Harlie, a Digital Marketing Specialist with a background in Culinary Arts. After completing my degree at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta, I moved from the patisserie world into digital marketing, building a solid foundation through intensive training at Purwadhika Digital Technology School.

My expertise spans SEO, social media strategy, performance-based advertising, content creation and e-commerce. With these skills, I design strategies that are data-driven, creative and tailored to deliver measurable results.

My culinary background sharpened my eye for detail and aesthetics, qualities I now apply to campaigns that are visually engaging and strategically impactful.

Think of me as a digital chef: I combine the right ingredients (creativity, precision and analytics) to serve marketing solutions that reach the right audience and drive real growth.
